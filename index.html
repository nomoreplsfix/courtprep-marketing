How it works.

01

Walk through the courtroom.

A guided tour of the room, so you know who is who and where to sit.

02

Start a session.

Begin a hearing with our digital judge, and, where it helps, a participant from the other side.

03

Turn on your camera, when you feel ready.

You should only enable your camera if you feel ready. This is just so we can also help you practise holding strong, steady eye contact.

04

See feedback on your delivery.

How your delivery came across, on pace, clarity and composure. Never on what you should say.

05

Receive reminders before the day.

Simple reminders of what to have in mind before the hearing, so nothing important slips on the day.
